PART L — FAQ (Jo Har Student Poochta Hai)

Q: 2nd year me enroll karne ka fayda kya hai? 3rd year se bhi toh kar sakta hoon? A: 3rd year me karoge toh 18 mahine ka kaam 12 mahine me fit karna padega — jaldi hi 90% log race me peeche. 2nd year = comfortable pace + pehle internships + placement season pe full confidence.

Q: Agar ek mahine miss ho jaye (exams, family reason)? A: 2 pauses allowed (30–30 days) + streak freezes (2/month) + Sem Exam Week Mode. Kuch nahi bigadta.

Q: Guarantee milega placement? A: Nahi. Aur koi honest platform guarantee nahi de sakta. Hum: 2 saal ka structured system + consistent practice + visible progress + interview-level preparation. Effort aapki, system humari. Ye baat pehle clear, baad me nahi.

Q: Non-CS branch (Mech/ECE/EEE) ke liye fit hai? A: Haan — core branch subjects (semester support) + aptitude + DSA + CS basics included. Service companies 80% branches se leti hain.

Q: Kitna daily time? A: 1.5–2 ghante weekdays, 3–4 ghante weekend. Consistency > intensity.

Q: Course baad me access rahega? A: Purchase se 24 mahine ya placement confirm tak. Extension available. Certificates lifetime.

Q: Doubt kaise solve karunga? A: Weekly live doubt session + cohort community + assignment feedback. 1:1 mock interviews me direct expert se baat.

Q: Mobile pe chalega? A: Haan — mobile-first design. Contests, lessons, assignments — sab mobile pe.

Q: Agar mujhe placement nahi karni, higher studies (GATE/MS) karni hai? A: CS core + aptitude + DSA foundation GATE ke liye bhi kaam aata hai. Path ka Phase 1–2 GATE foundation bhi strong karta hai.

Q: Refund policy? A: 7 din ke andar, ≤10% content consume → full refund. Uske baad nahi (system Day 1 se chalu). Clear, hidden nahi.
