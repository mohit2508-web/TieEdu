# PART A — KYA GALAT HO RAHA HAI (Aur Kaise Nahi Hoga)

## Problem: Placement ki taiyari kyun fail hoti hai?

| # | Real Problem | Root Cause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"DSA shuru kar diya, but kaha tak pahunca koi idea nahi"* | Koi structured milestone nahi, sirf random YouTube playlists |
| 2 | *10 subscriptions — DSA ka alag, aptitude ka alag, mock ka alag* | Koi ek jagah nahi, sab scattered, progress bhi fragmented |
| 3 | *3rd year end me panic — "ab kya karun?"* | Shuruaat late — 18 mahine ka kaam 6 mahine me fit nahi hota |
| 4 | *Self-paced course kharida, 3 mahine me band* | Cohort nahi, accountability nahi, deadline nahi = dropout |
| 5 | *Resume interview se 1 din pehle banta hai* | Projects, GitHub, LinkedIn — sab last moment pe |
| 6 | *Mock interview ka pata hi nahi tha offer aane se pehle* | Real interview = first interview = 50% chances khatam |
| 7 | *Service company vs product company ka farak pata hi nahi* | Har jagah same content — target hi clear nahi |

## Solution: Placement Prep Path

Ek **2 saal ka preparation operating system** — jisme har hafte kya karna hai, wo pehle se decided hai. Aap sirf follow karo; system baaki sab track karega.

```
❌ BINA PATH                    ✅ WITH PATH
─────────────────                ─────────────────
Kya padhna hai? — pata nahi  →   Har hafte ka plan ready
Kitna time dena hai? — guess →   1.5–2 ghante/day fixed rhythm
Progress kaisa hai? — nahi   →   Monthly report card + skill radar
Interview ready? — pata nahi →   Phase gates + mock interviews
Accountability? — akela      →   Cohort batch + streak + leagues
Resume kab banega? — pata nahi → Resume v0 (Month 1) se hi
```
