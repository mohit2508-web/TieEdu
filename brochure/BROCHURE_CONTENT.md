# PAGE 3 · THE PROBLEM

**Layout Note:** Headline top-left. 2×2 problem cards (numbered 01–04, each with coloured top-edge accent: sky / amber / mint / navy). Callout box. Then a 3-column comparison table. Bottom: 3 dashed stat placeholders for campus data.

**Eyebrow:** `01 · The Problem`

**Headline:**
# Placement cells run on guesswork. Students run on forwarded PDFs.

**Lede:**
> Every campus has the same four problems. None of them are solved by another brochure, another seminar, or another shared drive.

**Four problem cards:**

**01 — The content is unverified**
A "Round 2 question bank" circulating in a batch group is often three placement seasons old. Students revise the wrong syllabus for the wrong round — and nobody in the cell can prove which is which.

**02 — Zero cohort visibility**
A TPO cannot answer the only question that matters on drive day: *how many of my students are actually ready for this company's round 2?* Attendance sheets do not tell you.

**03 — Every batch restarts from zero**
Each senior cohort discovers the process, writes their own notes, and leaves. The knowledge never survives the batch handover, so the institution pays to rebuild the same asset every single year.

**04 — Last-minute panic**
Twenty-four hours before the drive, "what should I revise" has no answer. Revision without a priority order is just re-reading — and the highest-yield topics are exactly the ones nobody flags.

**Callout:**
> The institution is not short of intent. It is short of a system that can prove **who is ready, for what, and by when.**

**Table — Symptom → consequence → what ready looks like:**

| What the cell sees today | What it costs the batch | What ready looks like |
|---|---|---|
| A question bank with no date on it | Students revise the wrong round, lose hours to it, and walk into the interview less confident than when they started. | Every question carries a source and a visible fact-check date. |
| Readiness tracked on paper, or not at all | Weak rounds surface only after the company has already rejected part of the batch — far too late to fix. | Readiness per company and per round, visible before the recruiter mail lands. |
| Notes rebuilt from zero every batch | The institution pays to recreate the same asset in every senior cohort, and quality depends on whoever wrote the notes. | One platform, reviewed once, reused by every cohort that follows. |

**Security & governance checklist:**

- **Server-authoritative commerce.** The client cannot set a price; the same function that builds the catalogue is the one that charges it.
- **Licensed PDF delivery.** Guides render in an in-app reader with a per-page licence watermark. Download, print and save paths are blocked server-side.
- **Signed certificates.** Ed25519 signatures with a published public key — verifiable by a third party, not by us.
- **Role-based access.** Separate admin, campus and student permissions, with an audit log on privileged actions.
- **Session hygiene.** Short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens with reuse detection, bcrypt cost 12, helmet headers, CORS policy and rate limiting.
- **Protected infrastructure.** Standard HTTP hardening headers on every route, plus device and install tracking for push delivery.

**Callout:**

**Layout Note:** Two-column FAQ list, each Q in bold navy, A in body text, thin dividers. Clean, scannable, no cards needed.

**Headline:**
# FAQ — the ones we get on every walkthrough call.

**Q1. Is this another "course platform" we have to market to students?**
No. The free tracks require no selling — they are free with no trial wall. The paid vaults are student-purchased at published prices (₹99–₹249). The institution's programme fee covers the console, onboarding and support, not student subscriptions.

**Q2. Who owns the content — us or TieEdu?**
TieEdu owns and maintains the platform and vault content. Your institution gets licensed access for the contracted period, plus exportable cohort data at any point.

**Q3. What stops students from screen-shotting and re-sharing the PDFs?**
Every page of every guide carries a per-page licence watermark tied to the reader session. Download-by-link, print and save paths are blocked server-side. Re-shared pages are traceable.

**Q4. How is this priced for students — and can our cell see what they pay?**
Pricing is a public 4-tier ladder computed server-side: ₹99 / ₹169 / ₹219 / ₹249. The same function that builds the catalogue is the one that charges it. There is no negotiation, no coupon theatre, and no hidden MRP. Cells see the ladder, not individual student transactions.

**Q5. Do we get individual student data / marks?**
You get cohort-level readiness analytics — who opened which vault, how far into the eight sections, and per-round readiness percentages. Individual study behaviour stays with the student; the console shows what the cell needs to act on.

**Q6. What happens after the drive — is the content still available?**
Unlocked vaults are the student's permanently. No rent, no renewal, no expiring licence. Free tracks stay free forever.

**Q7. Can we run this for pre-final years too?**
Yes. Most Department-tier campuses onboard both pre-final and final-year cohorts — pre-final years build foundations on the free tracks, final years drill target companies.

**Q8. How do we know it is working?**
Three signals: vault-open rates, per-round readiness percentages before each drive, and the post-drive readiness report. All computed from live data — none typed in by hand.

**Q9. Is there an offline story for low-connectivity campuses?**
The app installs as a PWA and downloaded guides stay readable offline — built for patchy campus networks.

**Q10. What does the free demo actually show?**
Twenty minutes, live: the cohort console with a sample department's data, one company vault end-to-end, the student study-plan generator, and the public certificate verify page. No cost, no commitment — bring your placement calendar.
