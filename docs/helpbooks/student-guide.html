1. Start in five minutes

  1. Sign up with your email. If your college uses TieEdu, follow your placement cell's instructions for joining, so your account is linked to your college.
  2. Tell us your graduation year on Today — it is the one question we ask.
  3. Pick a company you are preparing for and take its free readiness check.
  4. Add the test date if you know it. You get a day-by-day plan to that date.
  5. Do today's daily challenge — ten minutes, three questions — to start your streak.
Install itAdd TieEdu to your home screen for notifications and a faster app. On iPhone this needs iOS 16.4 or newer.

2. Today — your home screen

  • Your route and next step: the one thing to do now.
  • From your college: e.g. “14 students from your college practised today”, and nudges from your placement cell.
  • This week: your streak, lessons, tests and points, and your test dates.
  • Continue where you left off, last 7 days, and your companies with readiness for each.

Streaks

A day counts when you finish a lesson, a test or the daily challenge. Miss one day in a week and a freeze covers it automatically.

3. Companies — prepare round by round

Each company page (/companies) shows its rounds in order — online test, technical, HR and so on — with what each round asks, notes and worked examples, and a skill test in the real pattern.

  • Readiness per round and overall comes from your tests. Your weakest round is the one to fix next.
  • Every figure says whether it is checked or being verified.
  • From your college: past results and notes from seniors, if your placement cell has added them.
  • Compare two companies side by side.
  • The full pack for a company is paid; free previews show what is inside. Some packs carry an accuracy guarantee: if a round's pattern was different from what we showed, you can claim within 30 days (see /guarantee).

5. Plan

  • Test dates (/calendar): add a company's test date to get a countdown and a day-by-day plan. Each entry also links to that company's study group.
  • Study plan (/study-plan): a week-by-week plan across your courses.
  • Drive-day mode: on the day itself — a documents checklist, a 15-minute revision and a one-minute breathing exercise.

6. Together

Study groups

Join the group for a company you are preparing for (/study-groups). You appear by first name or initials — never your email or college. Members see who practised this week, and can send each other one of three friendly preset nudges. You can turn nudges off or leave at any time.

Peer mock interviews

  1. One of you taps Get a code and shares the 6-character code.
  2. The other enters it. Talk in person or on a call.
  3. The interviewer asks three questions and rates each answer on content, clarity and confidence, with an optional note.
  4. Both earn points (30 for interviewing, 20 for being interviewed) — at least 5 minutes, up to 2 sessions a day, once a day with the same friend. Then swap.

Leaderboards

Your college has its own weekly leaderboard (/college-leaderboard). You are on it only if you turn it on, by first name or initials. It restarts every Monday.

7. Getting hired

Get found by recruiters

At /talent you can let recruiters TieEdu has checked find you by your tested skills. They see an anonymous Candidate #… card: your scores, certificates, and the branch, year and city you add. Your name and email are shared only with a recruiter whose request you accept, and you can stop showing at any time.

Offer wall

Read how seniors got their offers, and share yours once you are placed (/offers). Stories appear after review, under the name you choose.

Is this job offer real?

Paste a message into the offer checker (/offer-check) to see the warning signs of a fake offer — for example, being asked to pay a “security deposit”. Real employers never ask you to pay.

8. After a rejection

A no is information, not a verdict. At /debrief note which round you reached and what went wrong, and get a plan of three to five next steps for the next company. Your note stays private.

9. Parents — sharing your progress

  1. On Today, under For your parents, create a private link. It shows your weekly effort, streak, certificates and readiness — never your answers or when you log in.
  2. If you like, tick Let my parent get a weekly update. Your parent then signs up on that page for WhatsApp or email, in Hindi or English.
  3. Replace or turn off the link at any time; any updates stop at once.

11. Points, streaks and certificates

  • Points (XP) come from lessons, quizzes, exams, the daily challenge and peer interviews. They are recorded once per achievement, so nothing can be earned twice.
  • Certificates from courses and skill tests each have a public verification page you can share with employers.
  • Your Passport (on Today) gathers your skills, certificates and readiness into one card you can share as an image.
