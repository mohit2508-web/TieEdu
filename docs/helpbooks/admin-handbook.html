1. Getting in and staff roles

The console is at /admin; sign in at /admin/login. Only accounts with the admin role reach it, and inside, each person's staff role decides which tabs and actions they get.

3. Daily and weekly routines

Every day

  • Payment Verify: clear the UPI queue so students get access the same day.
  • Offer Wall: approve or reject pending stories (pending first).
  • Recruiters: review new applications.
  • Candidate Reports: publish or reject new interview experiences.
  • Guarantee Claims: if the guarantee is on.

Every week

  • Analytics: sign-ups, activity, conversion.
  • Experiments: read running tests.
  • Settings → Parent WhatsApp: this week's sent / failed (Sundays).
  • Drops: schedule the coming week's posts.

4. Payments, orders and coupons

  • Razorpay payments unlock automatically through the payment webhook.
  • UPI (manual) payments wait in Payment Verify. Check the amount and reference in your bank app, then approve to unlock — real money in, real unlocks out.
  • Coupons can be limited to items and given an expiry. Expired or out-of-scope coupons are refused at checkout.

The accuracy guarantee

Off by default (Settings → Accuracy guarantee). When on, a student with a paid unlock can claim within 30 days of their interview if a round's pattern differed from what the page showed. Claims arrive in Guarantee Claims:

  1. Read the claim and check it against other students' published interview experiences.
  2. Approve or reject, with a note.
  3. If approved, refund through the payment provider yourself, then mark the claim “refunded”. Nothing pays out automatically, and “refunded” is final.

5. Content: companies, packs, courses, study plans

  • Company Hub creates the company; Content Builder designs its page section by section, with a preview before publishing. Publishing a company page writes an audit row.
  • Page settings include “Offer a Hinglish version”; fill each section's Hinglish fields, or English shows.
  • Every figure on a company page shows whether it is checked or being verified. Record sources for figures — pages without sources say so.
  • Pack Editor holds the paid content; mark modules as free preview where you want a taster.
  • Courses hold lessons, quizzes and the final exam; certificates are issued from completions and can be revoked in Certificates.

7. Colleges and the TPO portal

Each college's placement cell works in the Campus TPO Portal (/tpo). The TPO Handbook and the College Leadership Guide cover their side.

Onboarding a college

  1. Create the college and invite its first T&P Head. These are done through the placement admin API (/api/admin/placement/colleges, …/colleges/:id/invites) — there is no console screen for them yet.
  2. The Head accepts the invite link and then invites their own staff from the portal.
  3. Import the student roster (and past drives) so students appear in batch readiness. For GLA the importer is npm run import:gla in the backend; it needs PLACEMENT_IMPORT=1 in production and runs in one transaction.
CarefulRoster files contain real personal data. Keep them in backend/seed-data/ and never commit or email them.

10. AI features

  • Students always get free, offline feedback: the mock interview checks answers against key points; the English coach checks pace, filler words and common slips.
  • With AI on, Claude adds a score and a better answer (mock interview) or tips and a polished version (English coach), up to the daily limit per student. Over the limit, on an error or a refusal, the free feedback is used and the student is told why.
  • Only the answer text is sent to Anthropic — never names, emails or audio.
  • Each AI-graded answer is billed to your Anthropic account. Set the daily limits with that in mind.

11. A/B experiments

The Experiments tab lists experiments defined in code. The first: the company page's main button wording (A = as authored, B = “See where you stand in 2 minutes”). Success = the student starts the free readiness check.

  • Press Start; each visitor then sees one version, always the same one.
  • The table shows unique visitors, successes and the rate per version, and a verdict. No winner is named under 200 visitors per version, or when the difference could be chance (p ≥ 0.05).
  • Press Stop to end it; counts are kept. To adopt B for good, change the button text in Content Builder.

13. Rules you must not break

  • Never show a number you cannot back. Unknown figures stay blank or say “being verified”.
  • Never loosen a privacy floor (5 or 10 students) or a consent rule without the owner's decision.
  • Never edit the XP ledger by hand; corrections are reversal rows.
  • Back up backend/data/db.json before any data repair.
  • Never rewrite git history or force-push without the owner's explicit yes.
  • Refunds are always made by a person, through the payment provider.
