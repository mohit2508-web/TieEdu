1. Getting into the portal

The Campus TPO Portal lives at /tpo on the TieEdu site. It uses the same account as the student site — one account per person — but only works for people your college has given a portal role.

Accepting an invite

  1. Your T&P Head (or the TieEdu team, for the first Head of a college) sends you an invite link: /tpo/invite/…. Invites last 7 days.
  2. If you have no TieEdu account, the invite page asks for your name and a password and signs you straight into the portal.
  3. If you already have an account with that email, sign in first; the page then attaches the role to your account. An invite can only be accepted by the account whose email it was sent to.
NoteEach failure has its own message — invalid, already used, expired, signed in as a different account. If you see “expired”, ask for a new invite; it is not a fault.

Signing in later

Go to /tpo/login and use your normal TieEdu email and password. The portal then checks that your account holds an active role for a college. If it says “ask your T&P Head for an invite”, your account exists but has no portal role yet.

More than one college

If you work with several colleges, choose the college at the top of the portal. Every screen, export and nudge applies only to the college selected. Data from one college is never shown under another.

The Dashboard

The Dashboard shows who you are, the active college, your role and the exact permissions your role carries. If you cannot open a screen, this list is the answer — share it with your T&P Head or TieEdu support.

3. Batch readiness

Tab: Batch readiness. Your whole batch on one screen: students down the side, the companies your batch is preparing for across the top, each cell the readiness score that student sees on their own Today screen.

Reading the screen

  • Companies are chosen automatically: the ones most students in your batch are preparing for (set a test date for, or bought a pack for), up to eight.
  • Readiness comes from each student's skill tests on that company's interview rounds. A blank cell means the student has not tested for that company yet — it is not a zero.
  • Segments tell you who needs attention:
SegmentMeaning
ReadyAverage readiness of 70% or more.
In progressPractising, between 50% and 70%.
At riskBelow 50%, or no practice in the last 14 days.
Not startedNo tests or lessons yet.

Exporting

The CSV export is built in your browser from exactly what is on screen, so it never contains more than you can already see.

Who appears

Students appear once they are on your college roster (imported by your Data Officer or the TieEdu team) and have a TieEdu account linked to it.

4. Your college's public page

On the Batch readiness tab, the Campus page card turns on a public page for your college at /campus/<your-college>, in your college's colour. It is meant for your students and their parents and tells them how to join.

  • It shows aggregates only, and none at all until at least 10 students have linked.
  • A company's average appears only when at least 5 students have a score for it.
  • You can turn the page off at any time.

5. Campus intel

Tab: Campus intel. Two things your students value most: what happened at each company at your college before, and advice from seniors who went through it.

Placement history

  1. Choose the company and the year.
  2. Enter how many students were selected, and if you know them, the CGPA cut-off and backlogs allowed. Leave a field blank if you do not know it — never guess.
  3. Add a short note if useful (up to 300 characters) and save. Saving the same company and year again updates it.

Students of your college see this on each company's page.

Year on year

At the top of Campus intel, the Year on year card compares your recorded results: students selected per year, the change from the year before, and a company-by-year table.

How it countsOnly recorded numbers are counted. A company with no number shows as “—” and is not treated as zero. A change is shown only between two years that both have recorded numbers. The same figures appear in the management / NAAC report.

Notes from seniors

Placed students can leave notes for juniors. Nothing is shown until you approve it. Approve or reject each note under Notes from seniors; rejected notes are never shown.

6. Nudges

Tab: Nudges. Send a short message to a group of your own students — for example, everyone at risk in CSE, batch 2027.

Sending a nudge

  1. Who: pick one or more segments (Not started, At risk, In progress, Ready). Optionally narrow by branch and batch. The page shows how many students are in those groups.
  2. Message: choose a preset — Come back to practice, Start preparing, Company test coming up (choose the company), or Keep it up.
  3. Add a line if you like, up to 200 characters — for example “Mock drive on Friday at 10 am in Lab 3.” Links, phone numbers and email addresses are not allowed.
  4. Check “Students will see”, then press Send for approval (Officers) or Send now (Head).

The approval rule

You areWhat happens
T&P OfficerYour nudge waits for the T&P Head. Nothing reaches students until it is approved. You can withdraw it while it waits.
T&P HeadYour own nudges go out when you send them. Officers' nudges appear under Waiting for your approval: Approve and send, or Don't send with an optional reason the officer will see.

What students receive

  • The message, signed “<Your college> placement cell”, in the app and as a phone notification if they have notifications on. Nudges are never sent by email.
  • At most 2 college nudges per student in any 7 days. Students over the limit are skipped automatically.
  • Students can turn college nudges off. The portal shows how many have, never who. They are skipped automatically.
  • The audience is worked out again when the nudge is sent, so an approval a day later reaches the students who are in that group then.

The History list shows every nudge, who drafted and decided it, and how many students it reached, opted out or were over the weekly limit. Every step is also written to the placement audit log.

7. Reports for management and NAAC

Tab: Reports (T&P Head, Data Officer and Management). A placement readiness report you can preview on screen and download as a PDF.

  1. Choose the period (the default is the last 12 months) and, if you want, one batch.
  2. Press Update to preview, then Download PDF.

What is in it

  1. Students on TieEdu, how many practised in the period, and the readiness segments.
  2. Readiness by branch.
  3. Practice in the period: skill tests, lessons, practice days, certificates.
  4. Placement results by year, with the change from the previous year, as recorded in Campus intel.
PrivacyThe report contains totals only — no student names, roll numbers or individual results. Any branch with fewer than 5 students is combined into “Other branches” so no individual can be identified. Each download is recorded in the audit log.

The report is suitable as supporting evidence for NAAC Criterion 5 (Student Support and Progression). It does not calculate NAAC's own metrics for you.

8. Mock drives

Tab: Drives. Run a mock placement drive for your students in the real pattern — online test through interview — and see their results. Officers and the Head can create and run drives for the selected college only; drives of other colleges are never visible.

10. Privacy rules built into the portal

  • Students' answers and attempt-by-attempt scores are never shown — readiness, counts and last active only.
  • Names appear only to roles that hold the student-details permission (Head and Officers); everyone else sees roll numbers.
  • No college number appears anywhere for groups too small to be anonymous (5 for reports and company averages, 10 for the campus page and the Today line).
  • Nudges need the Head's approval, are capped per student, and can be turned off by the student.
  • Every nudge decision, report download and role change is in the placement audit log.

11. Troubleshooting and support

You seeWhat it means / what to do
“Ask your T&P Head for an invite”Your account has no portal role. Ask your Head (or TieEdu support for a first Head).
“Placement backend unavailable” / “College data is temporarily unavailable”A temporary server problem. Try again in a few minutes.
A tab is missingYour role does not include it. Check the permissions on the Dashboard.
“No college selected”Choose your college at the top of the portal.
A student is missing from batch readinessThey are not on the roster yet, or have not linked their TieEdu account. Ask your Data Officer.
“No students match that group right now”The segments and filters you picked contain nobody. Widen them.
A nudge reached fewer students than expectedCheck History: some had turned nudges off or already had two college nudges that week.

For anything else, contact TieEdu support from the address in the site footer, with your college name and the permissions list from your Dashboard.
